## Recommendation

- **1st — Ainsworth Street:** wins the #1 factor (space) by a wide margin, plus garden
  and a second room to work/host. Unfurnished and less "new", but those are factors 4
  and 2, not decisive. Most complete for a couple.
- **2nd — Dazeley House:** best modern + furnished + efficient combo; would be #1 if we
  prioritised new-build and not furnishing anything ourselves.
- **3rd/4th — Marque vs Petersfield:** Marque is more premium on amenities (gym,
  concierge) and only 6 min out; Petersfield is roomier with a real balcony + parking at
  the best price — confirm furnished status and heating type per unit.
