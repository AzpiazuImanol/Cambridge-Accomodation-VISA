## What the document covers

CAS · visa application process · supporting documents · financial requirements (incl. the
28-day rule, acceptable evidence, deductions) · differentiation nationals · biometrics ·
arrival in Cambridge (e-Visa) · dependants (n/a) · a **"What this means for us"** section with
action items (passport choice, TB test, Morgan Stanley 28-day funding, arrival timing).

## Key findings for us

- **Dual nationality:** apply with the **EU passport** (Italian – Imanol, Spanish – Luciana).
  Both are **differentiation nationals** → no need to submit financial evidence. Argentina is
  not on the list. Use the same passport for the CAS and the visa.
- **TB test:** residence-based; **Argentina is not on the UKVI list**, so most likely not
  required (confirm on the official page).
- **Funding (Morgan Stanley):** investments/securities are **not** acceptable — funds must be
  **liquid cash held 28 days**; sort this early and consider contacting the ISO.
